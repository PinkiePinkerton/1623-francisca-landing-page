Uncompromising Craftsmanship in the North Valley's Most Coveted Enclave

Nestled peacefully in the prestigious luxury enclave of Tinnin Farms, this 7,107 heated square foot estate exemplifies authentic Southwestern architectural grandeur. Soaring timber viga ceilings, intricately carved corbels, and hand-plastered surfaces frame an expansive residence built to entertain at an elite scale while preserving warm, intimate living spaces.

Surrounded by mature cottonwoods and lush, manicured lawns on a fully fenced 1.11-acre sanctuary, the residence offers exceptional seclusion and privacy. From the gourmet epicurean kitchen and five distinct fireplaces to the dedicated wine room and secluded primary retreat, every square foot reflects generational craftsmanship.

Southwestern Artistry

Massive peeled viga beams, latilla accents, and custom hand-carved millwork throughout.

Estate Sanctuary

Expansive covered portals, outdoor kiva fireplace, water features, and room for equestrian or pool additions.
